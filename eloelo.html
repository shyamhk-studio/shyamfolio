Eloelo Becomes Elo Live

Eloelo is a live streaming app. The brief was a new logo and brand language that put the “live” front and centre, so people would know straight away that it’s a live streaming platform.

I rebranded the app completely: a new name treatment, a TV-headed mascot with cat ears and an antenna, a new brand font and new brand colours. The old mark was a television with a coin. The new one keeps the television and turns it into a character. The mark comes in a full set of variants, in 2D and 3D, for every background.
