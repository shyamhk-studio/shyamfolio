Rebranding of “Eloelo” to “Elo Live”

The brief was to design a new logo and brand language for the Eloelo app. The main reason behind the rebrand was to bring the “live” element into the app and tell users explicitly that it's a live streaming platform.

I rebranded the app completely: a new name treatment, a TV-headed mascot with cat ears and an antenna, a new brand font and new brand colours.
