Novo: Brand Identity

Role
Logo design, logo presentation, launch posters and mailers, animation direction
Team
Glance brand team. I contributed to the brand book.
Year
2023

Novo is the news app on the Glance lock screen: breaking headlines and personalised updates, without unlocking your phone. It started as an unbranded news page inside Glance. This project gave it a logo, a brand book and a launch.

Brief

Design a logo for the brand, with the tagline “What’s New”. It had to sit comfortably beside the Glance logo and still stand apart from the news apps people already knew.

Final Design

The final mark rests on three decisions:

  • The name. “Novo” comes from the Latin novus, meaning new. News has to be served fresh, so being first with what is new is the centre of the brand.
  • The quotation marks. The letterforms are cut so that a pair of quotation marks appears inside the word. Quotes signal that something is worth repeating, and they became the brand’s main graphic element.
  • Lower case and orange. A solid lower-case sans serif keeps the brand friendly and easy to read. Novo Orange (#FF5000) is warm and confident, and it stands out on a lock screen full of photographs.

The Logo Presentation

I designed and wrote the deck that presented the logo to stakeholders in June 2023, built on the Glance presentation template. It walks through the story of the name, the visual element, the typeface and the colour before showing the mark.

Logo Animation

I directed the logo animation: the quotation marks arrive first and the rest of the word forms around them.

Brand Book

The logo grew into a 46-page brand book, which I worked on with the Glance brand team. It covers clear space, co-branding with Glance, colour, typography, iconography, the quotation-mark element and tone of voice.

Launch

For the internal launch in September 2023 I designed the posters and the mailer series: two teasers, the reveal and the launch-day announcement. The line was “Bringing life back to news”.

Impact

After Novo moved from an unbranded news page on Glance to a product with its own identity, the numbers per article rose.
