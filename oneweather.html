1Weather Brand Launch Campaign

1Weather is a hyperlocal weather forecast app with over 100 million users. For its brand launch I designed the key visuals and the marketing collateral, such as posters, within the brand guidelines.

The campaign line, “Own the day”, frames the forecast as a way to plan your day better, whether that means fresh snow, a beach day, or leaving the office before the rain.

Impact

In the month after the “Own the Day” launch campaign:

50,000

Downloads in the first month, about 200% higher than before the campaign

+25%

Daily active users after the campaign went live
