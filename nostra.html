Nostra: Brand, Reports, Web and Social

Nostra is the gaming side of the Glance lock screen: hundreds of games you can play without downloading anything, plus live streams, tournaments and gaming communities, reaching over 200 million people across India and Southeast Asia.

I was part of the brand team. I made the brand document and guidelines, designed the Gaming Trends Report 2023, designed the initial look of the Nostra website, and was in charge of Nostra’s social media channel.

Brand Guidelines

The guidelines cover logo architecture and usage, the four-colour palette and its combinations, patterns built from the logo unit, typography, iconography, imagery and ad templates. The logo’s four dots stand for the four directional buttons found on almost every game, and each dot carries one of the core colours.

Gaming Trends Report 2023

I designed the Nostra Gaming Trends Report 2023: a 38-page report on how people play on the lock screen across India and Indonesia, written for game developers and partners. The job was to turn a long document of figures into something a busy reader could scan: one idea per spread, the key number set large, and charts drawn in the brand colours.

Website

I designed the initial look of the Nostra website: a mobile-first homepage that explains what makes Nostra different, one idea per screen. Each screen pairs one bold headline with a live phone mock-up and the brand’s diagonal colour bars.

Social Media

I was in charge of Nostra’s social media channel: event campaigns like Nostra at Gamescom 2023 and a Nostra and InMobi evening in Tokyo, plus recurring series for game developers, like the monthly Top 5 games and theme-based promotions.

When Nostra partnered with Samsung, I designed the announcement posts, exploring a few illustration directions for the same message.

Ads for Game Developers

Nostra also had to win over the studios that publish on it. I designed a LinkedIn ad series for developers, one benefit per ad, each backed by one number.

For proof, I built a case study carousel template. The first one told how the studio Bravestars reached 4 million unique users in two months: challenge, solution, impact.
